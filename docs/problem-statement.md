# Problem statement

## Context

The CL25M batch is the 2026–27 M.Tech Clinical Engineering cohort at IIT Madras: 15 students (roll numbers CL25M001 to CL25M016, with no CL25M014). During the placement season, students applied independently to many companies through different channels, including off-campus opportunities whose assessment dates had to be tracked alongside other batch activities.

## What went wrong without a shared record

| Difficulty | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Applications lived in WhatsApp conversations, personal notes and individual records | No consolidated view of who applied where |
| The same company was written many ways ("Commotion", "commotion", "Commotion Pvt. Ltd.") | Manual counts were unreliable |
| Assessment dates were known only to individual students | No central view of upcoming tests |
| Students' choices are personal | Sharing applications in a group would expose them |
| No figures on student interest per company | The placement coordinator could not show companies how many Biomedical and Clinical Engineering students were interested when asking them to open opportunities |

## Core question

> How can a small postgraduate batch maintain a reliable, centralised and privacy-conscious record of placement applications, company-wise participation and upcoming assessments, while giving its placement coordinator actionable visibility into the placement season?

## Constraints

- **Time:** the placement drive was already running, so the tool had to be usable within days.
- **Cost:** no budget; free hosting tiers only, no server to maintain.
- **Privacy:** classmates must not see each other's applications; names restricted to the student and the administrator.
- **Ease of use:** sign-in without email, usable on a phone.

## Scope (what the application does not do)

It records and reports applications and assessment dates entered by students. It does not track student locations, allocate examination halls, assign invigilators or provide proctoring.
